    How does dokuce.me work?

    Three simple steps from drop-off to delivery.

    1

    Shipment is handed to an agency

    The agency in your city creates the shipment for the receiver's city and you get a unique tracking code.

    2

    Status updates live

    The agency regularly updates the status — in transit, arrived, ready for pickup.

    3

    You track it in real time

    Enter your code on dokuce.me to see exactly where your shipment is, any time.

    Why dokuce.me?
